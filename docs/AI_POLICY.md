Adapted from [ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md) and [rust-analyzer](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust-analyzer/blob/master/AI_POLICY.md) policies:

- **The human-in-the-loop must fully understand all code.** You must be able to explain and defend every line of code when asked. AI replies not accepted.

- **Issues and discussions can use AI assistance but must have a full
  human-in-the-loop.** This means that any content generated with AI
  must have been reviewed _and edited_ by a human before submission.
  AI is very good at being overly verbose and including noise that
  distracts from the main point. Humans must do their research and
  trim this down.

- **No AI-generated media is allowed (art, images, videos, audio, etc.).**
  Text and code are the only acceptable AI-generated content, per the
  other rules in this policy.

- **We do not allow autonomous agents to be used to open pull requests or issues to our projects**.

This is not an anti-AI stance -- we use AI all the time! But this policy recognizes that quality-code and maintainer time are valuable and scarce resources, and aims to ensure they are treated as such, by LLMs and humans alike.
