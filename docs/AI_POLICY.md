Adapted from [ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md) and [rust-analyzer](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust-analyzer/blob/master/AI_POLICY.md) policies:

- **The human-in-the-loop must fully understand all code.** You must be able to explain and defend every line of code when asked. AI replies not accepted.

This is not an anti-AI stance -- we use AI all the time! But this policy recognizes that quality-code and maintainer time are valuable and scarce resources, and aims to ensure they are treated as such, by LLMs and humans alike.
